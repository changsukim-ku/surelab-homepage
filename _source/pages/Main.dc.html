Representations for superintelligence

SuRe Lab studies how superintelligence should represent the world, including ordered and compact representations. Our work spans order learning, learned compression for humans and machines, and visual understanding, published at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.
