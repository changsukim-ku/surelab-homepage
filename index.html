Representations for superintelligence.

SuRe Lab studies how AI should represent the world: ordered, compact, and ready for decisions. Our work spans order learning, learned compression for humans and machines, and visual understanding, published at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.

Research

Order Learning

Estimating ranks by comparing instances with references of known ranks, with applications to age estimation and image quality assessment.

OL (ICLR 2020) · GOL (NeurIPS 2022) · UOL (ICLR 2024) · ConOrd, SOL (ICML 2026)

Learned Compression

Learned image and video codecs for humans, and compact representations that let AI models understand visual data from fewer tokens.

DPICT (CVPR 2022) · CTC (CVPR 2023) · BLI (NeurIPS 2026)

Visual Understanding

Image quality assessment, enhancement, frame interpolation, lane detection, depth estimation, and segmentation.

BiFormer (CVPR 2023) · MFP, MSPN (CVPR 2024) · CanonCGT (CVPR 2026)
