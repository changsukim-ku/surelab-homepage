Graduate admissions · September 2027 / March 2028

Join SuRe Lab.

We are recruiting M.S., Ph.D., and integrated M.S.–Ph.D. students to start in September 2027 or March 2028. International students are welcome.

What we do

Order Learning

How should a machine represent things that come in order, such as age, quality, or preference? We introduced order learning at ICLR 2020 and keep developing it.

Learned Compression

How few tokens does an AI need to understand an image or a video? We build learned codecs for humans and for machines.

Visual Understanding

Quality assessment, enhancement, frame interpolation, depth, lanes, and segmentation: the problems where these representations are put to work.

Who should apply

  • You want to do research, not just earn a degree.
  • You are motivated, and you enjoy tackling challenging problems.
  • You aim to write your own papers for CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.
  • You are comfortable with mathematics and programming, or ready to become so.

Bring your motivation. We have the challenging problems.

What you get

  • Close, one-on-one supervision from Prof. Chang-Su Kim, IEEE Fellow.
  • Weekly seminars, and mock reviews of every paper by labmates before submission.
  • A track record: 26 Ph.D. graduates, now at Amazon, Samsung, Qualcomm, and five Korean universities as faculty.

How to apply

Email Prof. Chang-Su Kim with your CV, transcripts, and a one-page statement of the research you want to do. Admission follows the Korea University Graduate School schedule, so contact us early.
